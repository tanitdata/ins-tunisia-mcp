# Data attribution & licence

Data served by this tool is produced by the **Institut National de la Statistique (INS),
Tunisie** and retrieved from the INS data portal (http://dataportal.ins.tn/).

Use of the data is governed by the INS *conditions d'utilisation*:
https://www.ins.tn/conditions-utilisation (governed by Tunisian law).

It is an attribution-style open licence (CC-BY / ODC-BY compatible): commercial use is
permitted and no signed licence is required, but **attribution is mandatory** (Article 4).
Every reuse must carry:

- the producer name — *Institut National de la Statistique (INS), Tunisie*;
- a source reference — the portal URL above (or a per-source reference);
- the data's **date of last update**.

To satisfy this, every data-returning tool in this server embeds an `attribution` block
in its response (`producer`, `source_url`, `license_url`, `last_updated`, `disclaimer`),
with `last_updated` taken from the actual data returned. This block is a compliance field
and must be reproduced when the data is presented; it must not be stripped or altered, and
nothing should imply that this tool is an official INS product or is endorsed by INS.

This tool is **not** an official INS publication and is not endorsed by INS. Data is
provided as-is and may contain errors or gaps.
